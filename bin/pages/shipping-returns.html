Shipping

  • We deliver to all governorates in Egypt.
  • Shipping is paid to the courier when your order arrives: usually 80–150 EGP, depending on your area.
  • Cairo & Giza: 1–3 working days. Rest of Egypt: 3–5 working days.
  • We call or message you to confirm every order before it ships.

Returns & refunds

  • You can return an item within 14 days of delivery if it is unopened, unused and in its original packaging.
  • For hygiene reasons, opened or used skin care products can't be returned unless they arrived damaged or wrong.
  • If your order arrives damaged or incorrect, message us within 48 hours with a photo and we'll replace it or refund you in full, including shipping.
  • Refunds are sent within 7–14 days of receiving the returned item, to the same payment method or by InstaPay / wallet.

To start a return, contact us on WhatsApp with your order number.
